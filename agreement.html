Condition and Photos
Pre Move-In RecordLandlord will take clear photos and/or short videos of the studio and its contents before the tenant moves in, showing the general condition and cleanliness.
Start ConditionStudio is provided clean, functional, and in good working order, with furniture and appliances as agreed.
End ConditionThe studio should be left reasonably clean and in a condition broadly similar to the move-in photos, allowing for fair wear and tear.
Tenant Cleaning Obligations
  • Keep the studio reasonably clean during the tenancy.
  • Remove all rubbish and personal items at the end of the lease.
  • Return furniture and items to their original place where practical.
Landlord Obligations
  • Ensure the studio is reasonably clean at the start of the lease.
  • Ensure utilities included in rent are in working order at the start of the lease.
Use and Occupancy
Permitted UseResidential only
SublettingTenant must not sublet, licence, or rent out the studio (for example via Airbnb or similar platforms) without written permission from the landlord.
Noise & Behaviour
  • No illegal activities on the premises.
  • Respect neighbours’ peace and quiet, especially at night.
  • No parties or loud gatherings that cause complaints or breach building rules.
Shared FacilitiesBathroom is shared with other residents. Tenant must keep shared areas reasonably clean and use them respectfully.
Repairs and Maintenance
Tenant Responsibilities
  • Notify landlord promptly of any damage or issues.
  • Take reasonable care of furniture, appliances and fittings.
  • Replace consumables such as light globes and gas canisters used by the tenant.
Landlord Responsibilities
  • Ensure the premises are safe, reasonably clean and fit to live in at the start of the lease.
  • Carry out necessary repairs within a reasonable time after being notified.
Urgent RepairsSerious leaks, electrical faults, or anything that makes the premises unsafe should be reported immediately so the landlord can arrange urgent repairs.
Access and Inspections
Access SummaryLandlord may access the premises for inspections, repairs or emergencies in line with NSW notice requirements (e.g. reasonable notice for inspections, immediate access for emergencies).
Routine InspectionsRoutine inspections should be reasonable and in line with NSW guidelines (e.g. not excessive for a 10-week term).
NoticeLandlord to give reasonable written notice before any non-emergency entry.
End of Lease
VacateTenant must vacate on or before the agreed end date (15 February), unless both parties agree in writing to extend or vary the term.
Key ReturnAll keys and access devices must be returned on the final day of the lease.
Final InspectionLandlord and tenant may do a walkthrough using the original photos and any written condition notes to compare condition and discuss bond return.
Examples of Bond Deductions
  • Unpaid rent.
  • Significant damage beyond fair wear and tear.
  • Excess cleaning required due to the studio being left in a very dirty state.
House Rules and Building Rules
ReferenceTenant agrees to follow any reasonable building or strata rules that apply to the property (e.g. no smoking in common areas, rules for rubbish and recycling, quiet hours).
Key Rules
  • Dispose of rubbish in the correct bins and on the correct nights.
  • Respect shared spaces such as hallways and bathroom.
  • No storage of flammable or dangerous items in the studio or common areas.
